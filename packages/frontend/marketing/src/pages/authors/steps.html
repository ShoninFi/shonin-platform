How publishing works

Publishing, step by step.

{{i:seal:20}}Every step leaves a public record, including builds that fail.

  1. {{i:check:14:2.5}}Step 1 · Done

    Submit your code and pay $1

    Only a payment from the wallet that uploaded the code starts the job.

  2. Step 2 · Now

    The Reviewer reads your code and writes its report

    Six areas rated. Malicious code stops here, and is never built.

  3. Step 3

    An attested builder compiles it once

    It signs what came out. That signed build is the only one that can ever trade your vault.

  4. Step 4

    You activate it

    Choose its allowances and idle floor, and put a starting amount in the tank: at least $10.
