When you're done

Retire it with a week's notice. Change it by publishing a new one.

Retiring

{{i:clock:14}}A seven-day wind-down

  1. Day 0 · You retire it

    Deposits stop at once. The agent keeps trading, and Depositors leave as usual.

  2. Day 7 · Stopped for good

    The agent is stopped and its positions close. The cash goes home to whoever is still in.

{{i:concern:18}}Positions close at market, which can lock in a loss for the Depositors still in it. Their page shows the countdown from day 0.

Changing your agent

Publish a new one. Published agents never change: the build that was reviewed is the build that runs.

A new version gets its own review, build and vault. Depositors decide for themselves whether to move.

{{i:seal:18}}That's what lets a Depositor trust the page they read.
