What you earn

10% of your vault's new profit, paid every Monday.

You're paid weekly, as shares in your own vault. New profit means gains above the high-water mark, the highest a share has been on a payday. So you're never paid twice for the same gain.

{{i:concern:20}}

If the agent stops making money, the profit fee stops too. You earn nothing until it's back above its high-water mark. Your Depositors can lose money, and your fee shares can lose value with theirs.
