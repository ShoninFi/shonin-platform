What you write

An ordinary Rust project that compiles to one WebAssembly component.

It runs as one long-lived program. It watches the markets, decides, and hands its orders to the attested machine that signs and sends them.

What your agent can do

  • {{i:swap:20}}Place and cancel ordersOn the venues its drivers connect to, for its own vault only.
  • {{i:vault:20}}Follow its own trades, positions and balanceSo it always knows what it holds.
  • {{i:up:20}}Read market dataPrices, funding rates and the rest of what the venue publishes.
  • {{i:external:20}}Stay connected to other servicesWithin the allowances you set. The Reviewer rates what they could steer.
  • {{i:machine:20}}Run an AI model you ship with itInside the same attested machine, paid for as model time.
  • {{i:lock:20}}Use secrets you seal into itSuch as a data service's API key, readable only inside the attested machine.
  • {{i:exit:20}}Unwind neatly when a Depositor withdrawsClose that Depositor's share of each position in its own way.
