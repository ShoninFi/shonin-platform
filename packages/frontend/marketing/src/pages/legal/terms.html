What these terms cover

These terms are for shonin.fi, the app at app.shonin.fi, the docs at docs.shonin.fi, and the smart contracts and attested machines behind them. “We” and “us” means the people who build and run Shonin. “You” means anyone who uses any of it. By using Shonin you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use it.

Shonin lets Authors publish trading agents and lets Depositors put money into an agent's vault. Every agent is written by its Author, not by us. We run the platform: the review, the build, the machines the agents run in and the contracts their vaults live in.

Test networks only

Shonin runs on test networks today: Base Sepolia, Hyperliquid's testnet, Robinhood Chain's testnet and Solana's devnet. The dollars on them are test dollars with no value. Don't send real money to any address you find on Shonin.

Test networks are reset, slowed down and changed by the people who run them, and so is Shonin. Anything on them can disappear.

Who it isn't offered to

Shonin isn't offered to restricted persons. Every vault has its own restricted list: US persons and sanctioned persons on every vault, plus everyone the terms of the venues it trades on exclude. An Author can add to a vault's list, and nobody can take anything off it.

Before your first deposit into a vault, your wallet signs this statement. The vault's contract checks the signature and records it, and refuses a deposit without one:

I am not a restricted person for this vault, and I am not acting for one.

The statement you sign also carries the vault's whole restricted list, as the contract reads it: a comma-separated list of codes, such as CA-ON,SANCTIONED,US for a vault that trades on Hyperliquid. Each vault's page shows its list, and which venue each code comes from.

If any of these describes you, or you'd be acting for someone it describes, don't deposit. The statement is yours: we don't check anyone's identity, and signing it falsely is on you. Withdrawals are never restricted. Anyone holding shares can always leave.

The agent can lose money

The agent can lose money. Nothing here stops that. It could even lose it on purpose. A review isn't a prediction, and a track record isn't a promise. Before you deposit, read what can still go wrong: an agent can stop, a withdrawal can be delayed, an agent can be retired, a vault can be stopped, a venue, bridge or chain can fail, and our own code can have bugs.

No investment advice

Nothing on Shonin is investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and nothing on it recommends any agent. The review rates an agent's code. It never rates how much an agent might make. The agents named on shonin.fi are illustrations.

We don't promise that it works, that it's available, that it's free of bugs, or that an agent, a venue, a bridge or a chain does what anyone expects. As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for any loss that comes from using it.

What's yours to look after

  • Your wallet and its keys. We never hold them and can't recover them.
  • What you sign. Read what your wallet shows you before you approve it.
  • Whether using Shonin is allowed where you are, and any tax you owe.

If you publish an agent

Your agent is yours, and so is what it does. Publishing costs a flat 1 USDC, which covers the review and the build and isn't refunded: if the review finds the code malicious, or the build fails, the 1 USDC is spent and no vault is created. You sign the same statement as a Depositor when you activate, because your fee shares make you a holder too.
