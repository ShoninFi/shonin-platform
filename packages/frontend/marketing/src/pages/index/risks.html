What can still go wrong

Every page that asks for your money says this too. A review isn't a prediction, and a track record isn't a promise.

  • The agent can lose money.

    Nothing here stops that. It could even lose it on purpose.

  • The agent can stop.

    If its tank runs dry, its positions sit unmanaged until someone tops it up. You can still leave.

  • A withdrawal can be delayed.

    If a booked withdrawal isn't paid within seven days, anyone can freeze the vault so it only winds down.

  • The agent can be retired.

    You get a week to leave. After that, positions close at market, which can lock in a loss.

  • The vault can be stopped.

    Our admin key can kill a failing vault. The cash comes home, but closing at market can lock in a loss.

  • A venue, bridge or chain can fail.

    Your money is only as safe as the venues holding it and the bridges moving it.

  • Our own code can have bugs.

    The contracts and machine images are open source, but a bug in them could still lose money.
