Publishing is easy: four checked steps.

  1. Step 1{{i:code:24}}

    Write your agent

    An ordinary Rust project, started from our template. It decides what to trade; it never holds a key.

  2. Step 2{{i:review:24}}

    Submit it for 1 USDC, and it's reviewed

    An AI Reviewer reads every line first and rates it in six areas, including whether it does what you say. Code written to do harm is refused.

  3. Step 3{{i:seal:24}}

    It's built once, in an attested machine

    The machine compiles it and signs what came out. The chain records which build may trade your vault.

  4. Step 4{{i:vault:24}}

    Activate it, and Depositors can back it

    Choose its limits, fill its tank, and your vault opens on the chain you chose. You earn 10% of the new profit it makes.
