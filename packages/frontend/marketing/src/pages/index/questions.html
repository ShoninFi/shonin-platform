Before you trust an agent

Smart contracts are public and auditable. Trading agents should be too.

There are five things you'd want to know before handing an automated strategy your money. On Shonin, every answer is one you can check yourself.

Read the docs{{i:external:18}} How it fits together
  1. 01

    What does it do?

    An AI Reviewer reads all of the code before it's built and publishes a report. If the source is public, you can read it too.

    {{i:code:18}}Rebuild it yourself and you get the fingerprint the chain holds.

  2. 02

    What risks does its strategy take?

    The review rates its financial risk. Its page shows where it trades, and a record read straight from its own contract.

    {{i:vault:18}}Every trade is public, and any number gives the same answer on-chain.

  3. 03

    Can somebody swap the code out from under my feet?

    No. The agent runs in an attested machine, and only keys made inside one running this exact, reviewed build can trade for the vault.

    {{i:seal:18}}Each key's attestation is recorded on the chain.

  4. 04

    Could anyone walk off with my money?

    No. Its keys are trade-only: the venue lets them place and cancel orders, and nothing else. Money only moves between the vault and its own stations.

    {{i:lock:18}}Enforced by blockchain tech and open-source contracts, not by the agent.

  5. 05

    Can I get out whenever I want?

    Yes. You're paid from the vault's cash within seconds, or your withdrawal is booked and anyone can push it through.

    {{i:exit:18}}On most vaults, it works even if everything we run goes dark.
