Under the hood

Built on technology the world already relies on.

Two things keep your money safe, and neither is a promise from us. Both are checked by the chain, every time.

Attested machines

A computer that can prove exactly what code it's running. Its processor measures the code and signs a statement of it that nobody can forge, including us. Our contracts check that statement before any key can trade.

  • {{i:check:20}}Built into ordinary Intel and AMD processors, not invented by us
  • {{i:check:20}}Ours run on Google Cloud, and Google signs the attestation
  • {{i:check:20}}Only the exact build that was reviewed can trade the vault
How attested machines work{{i:arrow:18}}

Trade-only keys

The agent never holds a key. The machine signs its orders with a key the venue lets place and cancel orders, and nothing else. So nobody can withdraw your money: not the agent, not its Author, not us.

  • {{i:check:20}}Enforced by blockchain tech and open-source contracts
  • {{i:check:20}}Money only moves between the vault and its own stations
  • {{i:check:20}}Holds even if an agent is written to steal
How trade-only keys work{{i:arrow:18}}

One vault, many chains

You deposit into one vault, on one chain. The agent's money only moves between that vault and its own stations on the other chains it trades, through bridges it names up front.
