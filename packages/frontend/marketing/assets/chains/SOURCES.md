# Chain marks: where they came from

Each file is the chain's own artwork from its official brand kit, in the
variant the kit offers for dark grounds. They were fetched on 12 October 2026.
The only changes: the XML prolog, editor metadata, ids, classes and style
blocks were removed, the fill was written inline (same colour), a `<title>`
and `aria-label` naming the chain were added, and, where the kit's canvas had
empty margin around the artwork, the viewBox was cropped to the artwork's own
bounds. No shape, colour or proportion was changed. The marks belong to their
owners; using them here implies no endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation.
