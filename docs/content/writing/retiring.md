# Retiring

When you're done with your agent, retire it from its page in the web app. It can't be done in a hurry: retiring takes a week, so Depositors get a week's warning before an agent stops for good.

## The week, step by step

| When | Deposits | Your agent | Withdrawals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The moment you retire it** | Stop at once | Keeps running | Work as usual |
| **The next seven days** | Stay closed | Keeps running, as long as its tank lasts | Work as usual |
| **After seven days** | Stay closed | Stopped for good, and whatever it holds is closed | From the vault's cash, whenever they like |

**During the week,** your agent trades just as it always has, so Depositors who want out can leave in an ordinary market, not a rushed sell-off. It's still billed for what it uses, so keep enough in the [tank](../tank.md) for the whole week. If the tank runs dry, the agent stops early, and its positions sit untouched until the week is up.

**When the week is up,** the agent is removed from our attested machines for good. Its positions are closed and the money comes home to the vault. Any fees for that, like gas or bridging, come out of the money itself, so everyone still in pays their share.

## Afterwards

The vault stays on-chain for good, holding only cash, with no deadline for anyone. Depositors withdraw whenever they like, each paying their own transaction fee as usual, and so do you: whatever's left in the tank, and your fee shares, like any Depositor's.

A retired agent can't come back. To carry on with a changed version, [publish](publishing.md) it as a new agent.
